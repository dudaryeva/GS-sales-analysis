## Product Portfolio & Profitability Analysis

The analysis reveals a clear distinction between high-volume drivers and high-margin categories.

### 🔑 Strategic Priorities
**Cosmetics** and **Office Supplies** are the most critical categories for the business.  
They should receive primary focus as they drive the bulk of both:

- Revenue
- Profit

---

### 🥩 The "Meat" Paradox
While **Meat** is:

- The most popular item by volume
- A major component of the average basket

…it yields the **lowest profit margin**.

---

### 💰 Revenue Drivers
The core of the business’s turnover is comprised of:

- Meat
- Snacks
- Beverages
- Cosmetics
- Office Supplies

---

### ⚙️ Cost Efficiency
- **Office Supplies** exhibit the highest expenditure relative to profit.
- **Cosmetics** represent the most efficient category, yielding the highest profit despite significantly lower operational costs.

---

# 🌍 Regional & Channel Performance

### 🇪🇺 European Dominance
Business operations in Europe outperform other regions, characterized by:

- Higher profit margins
- Lower overhead costs
- Stronger operational performance

---

### 🌏 Asian Market
Historically, the business has seen consistently low engagement and popularity in Asia.

---

### 🛒 Sales Channels
There is no significant variance between **Online** and **Offline** channels.

Revenue, profit, and expenditure remain virtually identical across both distribution methods.

---

# 🚚 Logistics & Operational Constraints

Data indicates specific logistical bottlenecks and regional consumer behaviors.

### ⏱ Delivery Impact
Delivery time does not have a measurable impact on total profit.

---

### 📅 Lead Time Anomalies
- Orders placed on Sundays experience the longest fulfillment cycles.
- Georgia records the slowest logistics, with a minimum delivery duration of **7 days**.

---

# ✅ Key Takeaways & Recommendations

### 1. Margin Optimization
Investigate ways to improve the profitability of the **Meat** category, given its high popularity but low return.

---

### 2. Scalability
Double down on **Cosmetics** as the primary engine for high-margin growth.

---

### 3. Logistics Review
Conduct a root-cause analysis for:

- Sunday delivery delays
- The 7-day minimum lead time in Georgia

to improve customer satisfaction.

---

### 4. Market Strategy
Re-evaluate the value proposition in the Asian market or reallocate those resources toward the high-performing European sector.
